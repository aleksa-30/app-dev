**Knives Out (2019) - Movie Summary**

- Director: Rian Johnson Genre: Mystery, Crime, Comedy Cast:

- Daniel Craig as Detective Benoit Blanc

- Chris Evans as Ransom Drysdale

- Ana de Armas as Marta Cabrera

- Jamie Lee Curtis, Michael Shannon, Toni Collette, Christopher Plummer, and more

**Plot Summary**

Renowned crime novelist Harlan Thrombey is found dead just after his 85th birthday. Though initially ruled as a suicide, the eccentric detective Benoit Blanc is mysteriously hired to investigate further. As he delves into the twisted dynamics of the Thrombey family, long-held secrets, betrayals, and hidden motives come to light.

With sharp dialogue, unexpected twists, and a clever take on classic whodunit tropes, Knives Out keeps the audience guessing until the very end. It's a thrilling mix of mystery and humor that breathes new life into the genre.

**Why Watch It?**

- Brilliant storytelling with clever twists

- A stellar ensemble cast delivering entertaining performances

- A fresh take on mystery movies, blending suspense with humor
